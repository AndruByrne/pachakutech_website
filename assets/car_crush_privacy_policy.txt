
Privacy Policy

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. Our mobile application (the "Service") does not collect or store any images from your phone or device.
We will not collect any user data you do not positively provide.

---
Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or store any media from your phone or device, including:

Camera roll
Photos
Videos
Screenshots
Personal Data (e.g. contacts, email address, health data)

---
Data Collection and Use

Location Data:
- If you choose to place a trophy at a location, we will anonymously collect your location data. This data will be used to display the site of your high score on a map and to insert your trophy into other player's games.
- We use--but do not store--your location to deliver the appropriate high-score for your game.

We only collect and use anonymous, aggregate data for the purpose of improving our Service and providing a better user experience. This data may include information such as:

Device information
Operating system
Language preferences
Location

We do not share your personal data with any third parties except as required by law.

Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy at any time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through an in-app notification.
