Starting the app:
  Launch app from iOS or Android launcher. If you have not done so already, enter the organization code your manager has given you.
  Enter your user credentials on the next screen to login.
Uploads Screen:
  The top of this screen shows delivery records you have made recently and their upload status; dragging your finger down the screen will force the upload process to occur.
  The bottom part of this screen shows the delivery records you have successfully uploaded; storage is eventually consistent.
  The floating action button on the bottom right will bring you the camera screen.
Camera Screen:
  This screen is where you can record your delivery or audit by tapping the floating action button on the bottom right to take a picture.
  The first time you use this feature, you must grant access to the phone's location in order to include it in your record: press the "Fix" button in the pop-up message to resolve permission or status errors.
Logout: In the upper right are controls to reset account information.
